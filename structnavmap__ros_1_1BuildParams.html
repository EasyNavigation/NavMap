Detailed Description

Parameters controlling NavMap construction from point clouds.

from_points() uses resolution, max_dz, max_slope_deg, min_area, min_clearance, max_surfaces, min_surface_area and max_hole_area. max_edge_len, neighbor_radius, k_neighbors, use_radius and min_angle_deg are kept for API compatibility and ignored. For voxelized clouds, use a resolution of at least twice the voxel size.
