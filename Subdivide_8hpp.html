Detailed Description

Splitting NavCels: along straight lines (cut_along_lines) and around points (refine).

Both keep the mesh conforming (every edge is shared whole by the NavCels on both sides: no cracks) and carry what the NavCels hold: each new NavCel has the layer values and the surface of the NavCel it comes from. New vertices interpolate the position and the vertex colours. NavCel ids change; geometry accelerations are rebuilt.
