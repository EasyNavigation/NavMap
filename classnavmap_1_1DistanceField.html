Detailed Description

Distance from each NavCel to the nearest seed (seed NavCels and, optionally, the open edges of the mesh), kept up to date incrementally.

With a finite max_distance the field is exact for this definition: the distance of a NavCel is the straight 3D distance from its centroid to the nearest seed (point, or open-edge segment) that reaches it through adjacent NavCels all within max_distance of that seed, so it never jumps to another floor or across a hole. Each seed paints its disc; removing one repaints the seeds within 2 * max_distance of it (found through a spatial index). Without max_distance it falls back to NavMap::distance_field() (nearest-feature propagation, approximate) and updates recompute everything.

Bound to the geometry of the NavMap it was built from: rebuild it if the geometry changes. The map must outlive it.
