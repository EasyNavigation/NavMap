Detailed Description

Traversals over the NavCels of a navmap::NavMap: box, disc, ring, polygon, distance order and segment walk.

Every traversal calls a visitor per NavCel. The visitor may return void, or bool: false stops the traversal. Regions around a point (disc, ring, polygon, distance order) grow from a seed NavCel through the mesh adjacency, so they stay on the seed's surface and level: a disc on the ground floor never reaches the floor above, however close it is in XY. The box traversal is global (every surface). A NavCel belongs to a region when its centroid does.

Pass a TraversalScratch to reuse buffers across calls: once warm, no traversal allocates.
